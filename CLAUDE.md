# DWP Arabia website: rules for this project

See `README.md` for how the site is built and run, `CONFIRM.md` for open items, and `REQUIREMENTS.md` for status.

## Design and copy rules (from the site owner)

1. **No grid patterns.** Never use grid or graph-paper backgrounds (repeating line patterns, dot grids, perspective grids) in any section, hero, card or image.
2. **No dashes in copy.** Do not use long dashes (—) or short dashes (–) in any page text, headings, buttons, meta descriptions or alt text, in English or Arabic. Rewrite the sentence with a full stop, comma, colon or "and" instead. Hyphens inside words (e-invoicing, real-time, multi-currency) are fine. This also covers decoration: no short lines or bars drawn before labels or under titles, and no dashed connector lines, because they read as dashes.
3. **No AI indicators.** Copy and visuals must not read as AI-made:
   - no AI-style phrasing (dashes, "not just X, it's Y", "seamless", "unlock", "elevate", "in today's fast-paced world", stacked triplets of adjectives)
   - no labels or captions saying content or images were AI-generated
   - no sparkle or "magic" AI icons used as decoration
   - generated images must look like real photography or real product screens, never like obvious AI art
4. **The top menu is always part of the first section, on every page.** The header is transparent over the first section (no separate bar) and may turn solid only after scrolling. Dark first sections use the white logo and menu; light first sections (body class `top-light`) use the dark logo (`logo-dark.png`) and dark menu text until scrolled. New first-section types must start under the header (top padding of `--header-h`) and be added to these rules in `layout.php`.
5. **Always call it "Free Assessment".** The assessment tool is named "Free Assessment" everywhere (buttons, menus, links, text, emails), never "Free ERP Assessment" or "free assessment". Arabic: "تقييم مجاني". Only the spec-fixed browser title of `/sap-business-one/free-assessment/` keeps its SEO wording.
6. **No decorative numbers.** Never show counters or step numbers (01, 02, 03 or 1, 2, 3) on cards, tiles, steps, lists or tables of contents. Real facts with numbers (4,500+ employees, prices, phone numbers) are fine.
7. **Every page ends with the FAQ, then the footer.** The FAQ is always the last section (home page FAQ layout, with the same Riyadh skyline background revealed while scrolling), and the footer always opens with its "Let's talk" contact row. `faq_last()` in `app/lib/routes.php` enforces this; pages without their own FAQ get the home page FAQ. Do not put a contact/demo box between the FAQ and the footer.
8. **First sections never look like an "AI template".** No badge pills, floating glass chips, tilted 3D screens, glows, gradient text, animated blobs or looping motion in any first section. There are three kinds:
   - Home: `sections/hero-home.php`, the Riyadh skyline photo and video. Keep it as it is. Its tower lighting was regraded to look like a real night photo; do not bring back the neon blue arch. Live lights are transparent layers over the video (`img/hero/skyline-*.webp`, `.sky-layer` in site.css): the arch colour cycle, blinking aviation lights, and office windows switching off and on. They line up because the camera in the video never moves; if the video is replaced, rebuild them.
   - SAP Business One and the Cortex add-on pages: `sections/product_hero.php`, a mid-tone background (home skyline photo blurred under a slate-blue tint; the user rejected plain black), the real product screen on top inside a whole laptop (never cut off; the phone beside it when the product has a mobile app), and the wording below in two columns (logo + headline | text + buttons), all fitting one screen at 100% zoom. The user rejected for these pages: a light hero with floating cards, a people photo, a flat screen on a light background, a laptop cut off by the section edge ("half screen"), and an HTML-redrawn SAP Web Client. Use the real SAP Web Client screenshot (`screen_zoom` shows its left part larger).
   - Odoo, CRAS, PMS, Industrial IoT platform, Data and BI, AI, Industrial IoT intelligence, Consulting, SAP Business One implementation, support and ZATCA, and the four industry pages: the same product layout as the add-ons, applied automatically from `content/live/<page key>.php` by `content/live.php` (laptop screen first section, results, the page's own content, clickable process, live control room, works with, lead form, FAQ). Odoo is shown on its own, never with Cortex add-ons. Industry pages use their own first section (`sections/ind_hero.php`: industry photo and a band of live figures), not the laptop. Outside the SAP Business One area (its page, its service pages and the Cortex add-ons) do not add SAP Business One mentions, logos or hub logos (`no_sap`); say "your ERP" instead. Cortex AI sits in the Solutions menu under AI, not under SAP Business One.
   - All other pages: `sections/hero.php`, a full-bleed photo of people at work, plain headline at the bottom, one button plus a text link, and the facts bar (`partials/hero-facts.php`). Set the photo with `photo` (and `photo_pos`) or in the `$photos` map in `hero.php`.
